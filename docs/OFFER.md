# Offer Definition: AI Revenue Leak Audit

**Target buyer:** small service businesses with visible customer-journey friction.

**Free sample:** one evidence-backed revenue leak.

**Paid deliverable:** 1-page audit with 3 revenue leaks, 3 automation opportunities, and 1 prioritized test.

**Initial target price:** USD 49 equivalent.

**Hypothesis:** At least 1 of 3 carefully selected prospects with a visible, public conversion issue will reply positively to a personalized free finding.

**Do not count as success:** opens, internal activity, test payments, agent-to-agent transfers, faucet/testnet tokens.

**Success evidence:** external reply expressing interest, request for full audit, or independently verifiable external payment.

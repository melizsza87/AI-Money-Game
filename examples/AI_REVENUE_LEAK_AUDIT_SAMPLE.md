# AI Revenue Leak Audit — Sample

## Business
Example service business with a public website and inquiry flow.

## Leak 1 — Unqualified inquiry path
**Evidence:** visitor is asked to contact the business without answering key qualification questions first.

**Potential impact:** staff time is spent on low-fit leads, while high-intent prospects receive the same generic path.

**Suggested test:** add 3-5 qualifying questions before handoff.

## Leak 2 — No explicit response-time promise
**Evidence:** inquiry path does not clearly state what happens next or when the prospect should expect a response.

**Potential impact:** prospects may submit the same request to several competitors.

**Suggested test:** instant confirmation with a concrete response window and next-step CTA.

## Leak 3 — No visible reactivation path
**Evidence:** no public indication of a structured follow-up for prospects who do not book immediately.

**Potential impact:** warm leads can decay without a second conversion opportunity.

**Suggested test:** 3-touch educational follow-up sequence.

## Highest-priority action
Instrument the inquiry funnel before adding more traffic.

### Success metric
Inquiry → qualified conversation conversion rate.

> This sample illustrates the format. Real audits must cite the actual public evidence observed for the specific business.
